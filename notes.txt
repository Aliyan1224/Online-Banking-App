Core Features to Include (MVP) 
To make the project manageable, focus on these essential components:
Secure Authentication: User login, registration, and session management.
Dashboard: Displays account summaries, balances, and recent transaction history.
Funds Transfer: Peer-to-peer transfers within the application.
Transaction History: A complete, auditable log of past transactions.
Profile Management: Allowing users to view/update their personal details.
